THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

Precise below.
Playful above.

Keep the chart calm. Spend the energy on the moment that deserves it.

01 / FRAME

A quiet place for change.

Cool paper, a rounded white card, a light grid and restrained controls. The same chart stays with the viewer.

02 / CHARACTER

The endpoint is the actor.

A glance, a blink, a tiny recoil. Keep the character attached to its observation; move only its label when space gets tight.

03 / PUNCTUATION

One well-timed interruption.

Outlined headlines, an event card and a brief comic accent. Bring the camera closer, hold the meaning, then pull back.
