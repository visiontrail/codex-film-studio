# Provenance

General code-directed film principles were studied from [Lemo-Opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar): deterministic pictures, a common timeline, coordinated sound and iterative visual review. No upstream composition or renderer is included here.

The visual reference for the chart-interface vocabulary was [this animated chart post](https://x.com/kraton0903/status/2114257652930760928). This package does not redistribute its video, artwork, logos, music or captions. A reference's source credit is not a licence to redistribute its assets.

The guide and generic tools are independent implementations. New production-specific data, scripts, voices, media and review records belong outside the library.
