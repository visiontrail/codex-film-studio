03 / BEHIND THE REACTION

Draw the attitude.
Time the silence.

Read the style guide ↗
01

Two rhythms. One clock.

At 24 fps, hold facial drawings on twos where useful. Keep contours and resting poses fixed; use smooth, purposeful travel. Blinks and expressions carry a quiet beat without body or camera shake.

02

Give every cut a reason.

Establish a relationship in a two-shot. Use an insert to explain the object, and a close-up for the response. Carry space and motion across cuts.

03

Hear the punchline.

Contrast the voices. Put effects on the action, keep the synth score out of the dialogue, and make intentional silence truly quiet. Review picture, motion and listening separately.
