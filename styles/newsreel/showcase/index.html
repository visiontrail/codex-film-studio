A CREATIVE BRIEF TO COPY

Make a three-minute Newsreel film about [subject] for [audience]. Use relevant, credited archive pictures, warm monochrome, printed headlines, paper montages and purposeful period transitions. Write an original announcer script. Keep picture, voice, captions and foley on one timeline. Inspect the encoded frames, motion and audio; fix the problems you observe.

The reusable package is an authoring starter: a style guide, blank input contract and shared review tools. New scenes, a renderer and audio must be authored for each production.

05 / NOTES FROM THE BOOTH

Make the past legible.

Keep the date attached.

A publication date, an opening date and a filming date may differ. Preserve those distinctions in narration and captions.

Follow the physical picture.

A contact shadow stays with its paper. A page turns about a spine. Camera moves reveal the source without inventing missing detail.

Review the actual export.

Map comparable effects by action phase. Continuous frame review and full-size detail checks answer different questions; playback and listening need separate records.
