# Provenance

Style reference: [LemoLab / lemo-opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), [Newsreel reference film](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/newsreel.mp4). The upstream code is MIT; upstream demonstration films and stills are credited to LemoLab under CC BY 4.0.

The two published adaptations use their own narration and original scores. Archive pictures, print sources, TTS and audio licenses are listed in the independently packaged film credits. No reference soundtrack, dance photographs or reference story is copied into the new films or starter.

The independent page and schematic Canvas study are newly authored. The study's drawn city is a generic illustration, not a historical reconstruction. The style rules document the studied paper, camera and film treatment rather than claiming invention of that technique.

Locally served Limelight, Oswald and Noto Serif SC are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; their notices remain in showcase/fonts/. The font subsets include the page and programme text.
