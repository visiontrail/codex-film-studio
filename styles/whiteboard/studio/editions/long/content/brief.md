# Full-length director brief

Started 2026-10-21, Asia/Singapore, after the user accepted the short in
principle and requested people and recognizable national symbols.

Audience: English-speaking adults using AI to work and learn.
Question: What remains when the answer disappears?
Takeaway: Identify the skill, choose suitable help, then explain, correct,
change the problem and return later to check independent performance.

Target: approximately twelve minutes, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps. The 52 narration
segments contain 1,939 words, each within the service's 250-character request
limit. Natural-speed Chatterbox audio determines the
final duration. The timeline reserves room for visual pauses and the closing
pullback; an estimated draft runtime is not the final runtime.

## Argument

1. A worker finishes a spreadsheet with help, then faces an independent task.
2. Distinguish assisted performance, unaided performance, retention and transfer.
3. The Turkish mathematics experiment gives two different scoreboards.
4. Structured physics tutoring and checked mathematics hints provide counterexamples.
5. A one-week assessment shows why practice success and delayed learning differ.
6. Ask what cognitive work the learner is delegating.
7. Adapt support to current knowledge: example, partial step, fresh attempt.
8. Hide the solution, retrieve the method and correct the specific mistake.
9. Work through an original algebra example, including a deliberately wrong
   expansion that is crossed out and corrected.
10. Change the equation, identify near transfer and attempt a new one later.
11. Treat answer, hint and question interfaces as design choices, not evidence.
12. Keep a small learning log and return to the opening question.

The complete original research report is a read-only editorial input.
`sources.json` records refreshed primary-source checks and source limitations.
Practical examples and the learning log are editorial synthesis, not results
from a new experiment. Relative percentage arithmetic is explicitly invented
to explain the calculation. The small nuclear-safety study is described
narrowly and its current conference status replaces an outdated preprint label.

## Picture

One persistent board with twelve regions, arranged in a continuous snake
route. Drawings stay fixed while the camera moves. Use the same illustrated
learner across assisted work, unaided work and reflection. A red-hatched
Turkish flag locates the study; figures do not depict actual participants.

Keep the illustrated short's ink, pressure, surface, shadows, floating
markers and lettering. Use black for explanations, blue for help and
terracotta for independent work. Red is reserved for the flag.

The long edition needs more vertical room. As content accumulates, pull
back while retaining the heading and complete character. Do not move down
through a person's head. Keep focal text above the caption zone. Show the
entire board and tray at the end.

An erased answer leaves the learner and notebook visible. In the algebra
example, erase the worked solution while leaving the original question and
the learner's first attempt. The force diagram stays on one page of the
physics book; worked-example pages contain steps rather than empty outlines.

## Sound and timing

Use preserved Chatterbox jobs, original WAVs and the established voice at
speed 1.0. Independently transcribe every line without prompting the
recognizer. Bind final captions to the exact source and WAV hash.

Use the shared stroke events for marker foley, the explicit erase events
for felt rubbing and a quiet sampled score. Create a brief musical silence
at the practice/exam reversal. Do not stretch speech to hit a target duration.

## Acceptance

First review all 52 narrative stills and dense frames at native resolution.
Then inspect camera transitions, writing, flag hatching and erasing in motion.
After the final audio and picture agree, inspect every consecutive paired
atlas of the encoded film and record exact frame ranges. Technical checks,
atlas inspection, native-frame inspection and actual listening remain
separate evidence. No equivalence percentage is inferred from image metrics.

Preserve the delivered three-minute film and its hash-bound review.
The long edition has its own audio, timeline, render, captions and review
directory. The showcase offers it only after a complete delivery review.
Export both editions together after verification; update the process article
with actual results before public X publication.
