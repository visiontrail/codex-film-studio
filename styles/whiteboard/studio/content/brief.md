# Director brief

Production date: 2026-10-20, Asia/Singapore.

Audience: English-speaking adults who use AI for work and learning.
Question: What remains when the answer disappears?
Takeaway: Choose support that suits your current knowledge, then test whether
you can explain and apply the method independently.

This is an editorial condensation of the completed original explainer
`exp-8bd965b901ba40b4b734271f` (572.333 seconds, 167 scenes).
Its records are read-only inputs, not a production workflow.
User confirmed: complete and independently review the three-minute edition
first, without interim approval requests. Show it only after revisions.
The twelve-minute complete edition starts after the user's approval of this
edition. English narration; 1920 × 1080; 24 fps.

## Story

A completed spreadsheet hides a gap in understanding. Pull back to a question
written across the board. A line connects four measures of learning. Two
scoreboards expose the gap between assisted practice and an unaided exam.
A guarded tutor and a successful physics tutor prevent a simplistic anti-AI
conclusion. An answer card covers the learner's work, then an eraser clears it.
The same board becomes a practical sequence: attempt, seek the right help,
hide the answer, explain, correct, change the problem, return later.
Return to the opening question, circle the independent-attempt test, then
pull back to reveal the connected argument on the entire framed board.

## Visual contract

One warm, glossy whiteboard, dark ink #23262c, blue #2a5cb3 for help and
measurement, terracotta #d97757 for the learner's independent work.
All lettering consists of actual stroke paths, individually timed.
EMS Tech lettering and Architects Daughter subtitles, licensed separately.
Floating physical markers with cast shadows, caps and chisel nibs.
No hands. Drawings remain fixed in board coordinates; only props move.
Faint old lesson marks, scuffs, pooled ink, dry streaks and a broad window sheen.
Camera travels between regions, holds while reading, and reveals the framed
whole board at the end. Reserve the bottom subtitle region.

## Sound

Chatterbox at natural speed with verified text and preserved provider jobs.
Original quiet marimba/pizzicato/glockenspiel score using CC0 samples; a deliberate silence
at the reversal. Every pen stroke has a speed-shaped, spatially placed felt
sound. Magnet clicks, eraser rub, and cap sounds follow the picture's timeline.
Speech is primary; measure final loudness and peaks.

## Evidence and acceptance

Download and hash the exact reference film. Decode every reference and delivery
frame; save one CSV row per frame for appearance, occupancy, color and motion.
Generate complete consecutive-frame atlases with frame IDs. Record visual
inspection coverage explicitly and inspect suspect frames at full resolution.
Compare corresponding visual functions, not unrelated pixels of two stories.
Reject clipped focal labels, subtitle overlap, unexplained object motion,
missing writing strokes, blank frames, abrupt camera cuts, and unreadable holds.
Do not claim literal 100% perceptual equivalence.
