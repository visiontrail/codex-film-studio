03 / INSIDE THE STUDIO

Built from the
tip of a pen.

A reusable local film project: write a story, place it on one board, give every mark a time, and inspect the resulting motion.

01

People in the picture

A learner uses a laptop, takes an exam and thinks through a method. People and place cues are drawn with the same ink as the argument.

02

One shared clock

Letter strokes, camera movement, pen lifts, captions and felt sounds follow the same timeline. Every frame can be reproduced.

03

Evidence before polish

The script separates assisted grades from unaided exams, immediate tests from retention, and a helpful tutor from a ready-made answer.
