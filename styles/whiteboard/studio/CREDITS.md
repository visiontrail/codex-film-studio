Sample speech was generated with Chatterbox at natural speed. The Release
contains selected narration WAVs, hashes and independent timing for replay.
Service configuration and provider job records are not distributed.

The original 88 BPM score uses marimba and glockenspiel samples from
[VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) and a cello pizzicato sample from
[VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), by
Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and contributors. These instrument recordings
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Pinned source URLs, hashes
and licenses are retained in `assets/instruments/`. Felt, eraser and impact
sounds were generated from filtered noise and damped oscillators. The
reference soundtrack is not included in the new film.

Research claims and their primary sources are recorded in
`content/sources.json`. The spreadsheet opening is an illustrative scenario.
The suggested learning loop is editorial synthesis, with its limitations
stated in the narration.

Third-party runtime packages retain their respective licenses.

The illustrated edition adds original procedural learner drawings and a
hand-drawn Turkish flag. The flag's red field, white crescent and five-point
star follow the [Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Foreign Affairs description](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/the-turkish-flag-and-the-turkish-national-anthem.en.mfa).
The simplified drawing is not an official production template. No flag
image or external character artwork is embedded. The figures illustrate
learning situations and do not depict identifiable study participants.
