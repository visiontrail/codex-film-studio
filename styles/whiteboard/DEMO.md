Watch or download both films from the [v0.1.0 release](https://github.com/visiontrail/codex-film-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
Run `python3 scripts/fetch_demo.py --edition all` from the repository root to
download the films and original narration/timing inputs into this studio.

The source distinguishes assisted performance, independent performance,
retention and transfer. Studies and qualifications are retained in
`studio/content/sources.json` and `studio/editions/long/content/sources.json`.
People, books, computers, a calendar and a Turkish flag are drawn as timed
paths rather than composited stock artwork.

The release bundle includes the selected WAV recordings and independent
word timings required to reproduce the composition. It excludes credentials,
service addresses, provider job records and rejected development takes.
No TTS service is needed to replay this sample. New narration requires your
own audio generation or recordings.

See the root README for a complete build. The only available style in this
release is Whiteboard Explainer; the two running times are editions, not
separate styles.
