04 / INSIDE THE TOOLKIT

Build the material.
Then make it move.

MATERIAL TOOLKIT

A foundation for authoring.

Includes independent drawing, character, action and material helpers, plus an empty input contract. A reusable scene renderer, narration, score are authored for each production.

A /

Different constructions.

Indexed pixel buffers, character-built terminals, thin technical drawings, wax pigment and wet/dry ink. A material changes the drawing itself, not just the overlay.

B /

One clock. Every contact.

Keep cameras, commands and sound on integer frames. Inspect hand-to-tool contact, replay framing and small details at delivery size, then watch and listen to the final file.
