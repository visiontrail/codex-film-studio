01 / THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

Quiet field.
Precise reveal.

The camera discovers the space. A plane separates its layers. A single reading gives the image a reason to hold.

01

Keep the returns honest.

In a survey film, each point stays at its measured position. Sparse areas and missing returns are part of the evidence.

02

Make a layer do the work.

A scan front, a descending canopy cut, a surface in raking light. Each transition explains a spatial relationship.

03

Hold one reading.

Use a hairline section and an anchored label. Keep the source and method close to the number; label any display exaggeration.

03 / INSIDE THE STUDIO

From a return
to a reason.

A

Establish the ground.

Record the source, survey date, permission, classification, units and coordinate systems. Separate measured features from interpretation.

B

One shared clock.

Plan the orbit, cutting plane, labels, quiet interval and reveal together. Fine ticks and soft sweeps give a spatial transition a sound.

C

Review at the output size.

Inspect point clarity, occlusion, label holds and camera motion in the encoded film. Listen to narration and mix separately.
