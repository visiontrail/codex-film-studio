# LiDAR Survey · 点云测绘

Explore the [independent style page](https://visiontrail.github.io/codex-film-studio/styles/lidar-survey/showcase/index.html),
with English and Chinese copy, an interactive synthetic point-cloud study,
visual principles, creation steps and source credits.

The [style guide](STYLE.md) covers measured returns, canopy peeling, a surface
reveal, anchored sections, camera movement and sound. The [starter](starter/)
contains an empty survey and timeline contract. Source credit is recorded in
[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

Status: **starter-ready**. New films need their own verified survey data, scene
renderer, narration and soundtrack. The silent browser study is not a finished
survey film and its synthetic terrain is not a measurement of a real place.

Create an external project with `python3 scripts/new_film.py my-film --style lidar-survey`
from the repository root. Open that new folder in Codex and provide the subject,
audience, language, duration and dataset.
