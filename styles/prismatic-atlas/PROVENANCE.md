# Provenance

The directing principles were studied from LemoLab's
[Lemo-Opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), especially its
[directing guide](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/DIRECTOR.md)
and [technical guide](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/TECHNIQUE.md):
start from an argument, write an independent treatment, connect camera and sound
to one timeline, prove the look before exporting, and inspect the encoded result.
These are independently written method notes; no upstream demo composition is
included.

Visual reference: Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell,
[What Do Alien Civilizations Look Like? The Kardashev Scale](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rhFK5_Nx9xY).
The reference informs general color, staging, camera and motion analysis.
Its footage, soundtrack, characters and illustrations are not bundled or licensed
for reuse here. This toolkit is independent and is not endorsed by the reference
creators. A study of visual grammar is not a certified style-equivalence result.
