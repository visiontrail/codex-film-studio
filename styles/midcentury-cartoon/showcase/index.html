04 / NOTES FROM THE WORKBENCH

Keep the warmth.
Mind the details.

AUTHORING STARTER · READY TO BUILD ON

01

A method to build on.

Includes visual rules, blank inputs, print and timeline tools, and production, audio and review guides. Author the scenes, renderer and sound for each new film.

02

Let the hand make contact.

Fingers meet the actual switch, pen tip or handle. Check line weight and occlusion again in close-up, and leave the caption area clear.

03

Look. Watch. Listen.

Inspect exported details, normal-speed movement and the complete mix. Record what you observed and repaired; a finished render is the start of review.
