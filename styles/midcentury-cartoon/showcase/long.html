03 / INSIDE THE STUDIO

A paper world.
People with a purpose.

Research, demonstration and practice follow one thread: understand a step, try it independently, then test it on a different problem.

01

Let actions explain.

Ada closes an answer, removes tokens from both sides of a balance and checks her own solution. Warm paper, slight color misregistration, slab lettering and held poses shape the picture.

02

One shared timeline.

65 shots and 64 Chatterbox recordings follow one timeline. English captions, an original synthesized score and action sounds move with the explanation.

03

Keep the evidence precise.

Assisted scores, unaided tests, delayed retention and transfer are treated separately. Ada is a teaching character; the practical routine is an illustration informed by research.
