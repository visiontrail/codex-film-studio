# Provenance

Visual study: LemoLab, *Midnight at the Starlight Hotel*, Art Deco style demo in [lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), [released MP4](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/art-deco.mp4), CC BY 4.0, [@lemomo_ai](https://x.com/lemomo_ai).

The upstream animation code, scene composition, story, score and provider workflow are not imported. `starter/lib/material.mjs` is an independently authored Canvas 2D toolkit developed through visual reference study. It contains no production story or media.

Fonts named by the guide are Google Fonts under their respective SIL Open Font Licenses. A new production must obtain and retain their actual font files and license notices. No font binaries, reference video or third-party sound samples are bundled in this toolkit.
