# Provenance

Visual study: LemoLab, *Midnight at the Starlight Hotel*, Art Deco style demo in [lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), [released MP4](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/art-deco.mp4), CC BY 4.0, [@lemomo_ai](https://x.com/lemomo_ai).

The upstream animation code, scene composition, story, score and provider workflow are not imported. `starter/lib/material.mjs` is an independently authored Canvas 2D toolkit developed through visual reference study. It contains no production story or media.

Fonts named by the guide are Google Fonts under their respective SIL Open Font Licenses. A new production must obtain and retain their actual font files and license notices. No font binaries, reference video or third-party sound samples are bundled in the blank starter.

## Independent style page

`showcase/` contains an original, story-independent 16-second silent study,
bilingual documentation and schematic preview SVGs. It uses the existing
`starter/lib/material.mjs` vocabulary without importing any retained production
scene, narration, music or media. It does not establish full-film publication or
exact reference equivalence.

The page hosts unmodified Limelight (Sorkin Type Co, 2011) and Josefin Sans
(Josefin Sans Project Authors, 2010) font files under SIL OFL 1.1. Their full
notices are in `showcase/fonts/OFL-limelight.txt` and
`showcase/fonts/OFL-josefinsans.txt`. They were copied from the locally retained,
licensed font assets; fonts and page assets stay outside the new-film starter.
