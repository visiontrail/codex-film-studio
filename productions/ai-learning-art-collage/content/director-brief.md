# Does AI Help You Learn—or Just Finish?

Independent production; no FrontierTechSN Task or pipeline job.

Audience: adults using chat assistants to study or solve unfamiliar tasks.
Argument: assisted completion, independent performance, retention and transfer answer different questions. Choose the cognitive work that should remain yours, check explanations, then test a fresh attempt after support is removed.

Scope: preserve the full original argument in 46 narrated passages (1,657 words). English Chatterbox voice at natural speed; expected duration determined from generated audio. Educational studies retain populations, comparison and test limits. Spreadsheet and algebra examples are original illustrations.

Reference: 101 academy, Art Appreciation 101 episode 28, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=04UfoNpOQdw, 222.04 seconds, 1920x1080, 25 fps. Not a lemo-opuscar film. Actual reference footage determines visual constraints; lemo-opuscar contributes transferable craft principles only.

Visual language developed from full reference thumbnail review and selected native frames: tactile near-white cold-pressed paper, photographic magazine cutouts with coarse grain and hand-cut silhouettes, slight paper thickness and displaced shadows, black pencil scribbles, muted photographic colors with orange/teal accents. Detailed observations and remaining differences are recorded in reference-style-notes.md and the versioned review reports.

Delivery: 1920x1080, 25 fps, H.264 MP4 plus English SRT, original editable code and assets. Integer-frame authoritative timeline, deterministic state per frame. Speech, text, camera and sound all consume the same timing manifest. Each passage may contain multiple purposeful visual beats; no repeated decorated-slide template.

Review: every reference and delivery frame decoded and inventoried; all-frame atlases; separate records of actual visual inspection, motion playback and listening. No automated metric alone certifies a style match. Resolve specific observed defects and retain before/after evidence. The request for 100% consistency is a visual target, not a numeric claim to manufacture.
