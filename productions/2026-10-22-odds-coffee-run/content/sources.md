## Country-selection audit — 23 October 2026

The Turkey example originates in the supplied `一口气了解赌球.简体中文.txt`, lines 16–18. The original text says that 371 of 571 referees were suspected of manipulating matches. The TFF announcement of 27 October 2025 was reopened and checked: 371 referees had betting accounts and 152 had football betting activity. L027 and the on-screen card use those separate counts; L028 distinguishes accounts, bets and match fixing. This production does not adopt the transcript's stronger match-fixing claim.

The referenced “查找项目实例视频” chat concerns locating sample MP4s, not a Turkey case. The scene identifier `turkey` is confined to this production's story and shot selection. No country requirement was found in the studio's general director/technique instructions or methods. Cases must be chosen again for a new topic; never reuse these figures with another country's name or flag.
