# Leo the Farmer — a crayon storybook

An unofficial visual retelling of Play with Gyuri's linked farm story. English narration and short character dialogue, with time for a child to answer the vocabulary choices. Preserve the causal sequence: dress for work, feed everyone, milk, harvest, carry, rest. Reframe vocabulary photos as tangible wax drawings. Characters: Leo, golden lion, purple scalloped mane, white shirt, blue overalls, dark boots; Jojo, raspberry monkey, pale peach face and round ears, turquoise overalls and red boots. No source footage, recording, or music in the delivered film.

## Visual direction
Warm embossed rag paper; wax cracks, overlapping diagonal hatching, imperfect indigo lines; big readable eyes; hand-drawn poses on twos; continuous camera on ones. Material calibration uses the supplied crayon-book reference. Wide pages give breathing space, close shots show hands actually handling objects. Drawing-in, white-wax resist, and a curved paper turn are narrative events. No dashboard cards or synthetic gradients.

## Dramatic rhythm
Morning anticipation → sleepy hesitation → comic wrong clothing choices → busy animal chorus → quiet milk drops → effort and harvest → overflowing arms → practical containers → shared nap. Final silence then small music-box cadence.

## Delivery and evidence
1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264/AAC. Timeline in integer frames. Reference: all 1,248 frames decoded and indexed, all 35 atlas sheets inspected, selected frames at full resolution. Output: full sequential frame audit, every atlas sheet inspected, high-resolution checks at scene transitions, playback and audio checks reported separately. Numerical similarity describes materials and pacing; different stories cannot have identical pixels. Do not certify a subjective 100% match.
